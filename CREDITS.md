# Credits

## Background photos

All five backgrounds are **CC0 (public domain dedication)**, originally published on Unsplash and mirrored on Wikimedia Commons. No attribution is required; they're listed here as sources. Each is cropped to 16:9 and resized to 1920x1080; a pre-blurred copy feeds the frosted glass. The app adds its own time-of-day tint. The app icon and TV banner are made from the mountain photo.
